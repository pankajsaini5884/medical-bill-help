| HealthCare.gov insurers denied 19% of in-network claims in 2024; fewer than 1% of denied claims appealed; 66% of internal appeals upheld (KFF, published Mar 24, 2026) | https://www.kff.org/private-insurance/claims-denials-and-appeals-in-aca-marketplace-plans-in-2024/ (KFF's own share link uses /patient-consumer-protections/...; both resolve) |

| External review: file within 4 months of final denial; standard decided within 45 days; expedited within 72 hours; fee no more than $25 (none under HHS-administered process) | https://www.healthcare.gov/appeal-insurance-company-decision/external-review/ |
| ERISA (job-based plans): at least 180 days to appeal; plan must give free copies of documents relevant to the claim; appeal decision 72 h / 30 d / 60 d; ACA plans must give diagnosis & treatment codes on request | https://www.dol.gov/agencies/ebsa/about-ebsa/our-activities/resource-center/publications/filing-a-claim-for-your-health-benefits |

| Uninsured/self-pay: good faith estimate when scheduled ≥3 business days ahead or on request; dispute if ≥$400 over estimate; start within 120 days of initial bill | https://www.cms.gov/initiatives/your-patient-rights/medical-bill-rights/know-your-rights/without-insurance |
| PPDR: $25 non-refundable fee; no collections while dispute pending | https://www.cms.gov/initiatives/your-patient-rights/medical-bill-rights/get-help/dispute-medical-bill |
| Check bill for errors: ask for detailed bill, compare EOB, compare medical records, check duplicates, look up codes | https://www.cms.gov/initiatives/your-patient-rights/medical-bill-rights/get-help/guides-resources/check-your-bill-errors |
| Financial assistance: nonprofit hospitals must give FA to eligible patients; search "[hospital] financial assistance"; tell collectors you are applying | https://www.cms.gov/initiatives/your-patient-rights/medical-bill-rights/get-help/guides-resources/apply-financial-assistance |
| 501(r)(4): written FAP, widely publicized, free online/paper, plain-language summary, FAP-eligible patients not charged more than AGB | https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/financial-assistance-policy-and-emergency-medical-care-policy-section-501r4 |
