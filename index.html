How it works

  1. Find your situation. Wrong bill, denied claim, surprise out-of-network charge, or a bill sent to collections. The guide points you to the right letter.
  2. Fill in the letter. Add your account or claim number, dates, and your reasons. Keep it factual.
  3. Send it and log it. Mail or upload it the way your notice says, keep a copy, and record the date in the tracker.
  4. Follow up on time. The tracker shows what's due next, including when to escalate to an external review or a complaint.

A digital download: 15 fill-in letter templates, a 25-page step-by-step guide, and a deadline tracker. You fill them in yourself and send them yourself.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is self-help educational material written for patients. It is not legal, medical, or financial advice, and no result is guaranteed. For complex cases, a state consumer assistance program, patient advocate, or attorney can help.

Does it work for my insurance?

The letters are written for common U.S. situations: job-based plans, Marketplace plans, and hospital bills. Rules differ for Medicare, Medicaid, and some state-regulated plans, so always follow the instructions and deadlines in your own denial notice or plan documents.
