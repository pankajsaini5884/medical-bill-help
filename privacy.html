Privacy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This is a small static website. It is built so that we do not collect health information or personal information from visitors.

What this site does not do

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, advertising pixels, session recording, or third-party trackers.
  • No forms that submit to a server, no accounts, no logins.
  • No external fonts, scripts, or embeds. Every file is served from this site.

The free letter tool

The letter generator runs entirely in your web browser using JavaScript on this page. What you type is used only to build the preview on your screen. It is not sent to any server, not saved to browser storage, and is gone when you close or refresh the tab. If you choose to download the letter, the file is created on your device by your browser.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address in its server logs to deliver and secure the site. That processing is governed by GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or use those logs to identify visitors.

Buying the kit

When you click a “Get the kit” button you leave this site for Gumroad, which handles checkout and delivery. Information you give at checkout (such as your email and payment details) is collected by Gumroad under its own privacy policy. As the seller we receive the order details Gumroad provides to sellers, such as your email address, so we can deliver the product and answer support requests. We do not ask for, and you should not send us, medical records or health details.

Email

If you email us, we use your message only to reply. Please do not include medical records, claim documents, or insurance ID numbers.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change too.
