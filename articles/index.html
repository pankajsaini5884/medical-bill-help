Free guides

Plain-English guides to medical bills and insurance denials. Every deadline and figure is checked against official sources such as HealthCare.gov, CMS, the Department of Labor, the IRS and the CFPB.

  • How to appeal a denied health insurance claim

    A plain-English, step-by-step guide to appealing a denied health insurance claim: deadlines, what to include, internal appeals, external review and urgent cases.

  • How to ask for an itemized hospital bill and find errors

    How to request an itemized hospital bill, compare it with your EOB and medical records, spot duplicate or wrong charges, and get errors corrected.

  • Hospital charity care and financial assistance, explained

    What hospital financial assistance (charity care) is, the federal rules nonprofit hospitals must follow, the 240-day application window, and how to apply.

  • The No Surprises Act, explained

    What the No Surprises Act covers, what it doesn't (like ground ambulances), notice-and-consent forms, good faith estimates, the $400 dispute rule and how to complain.

  • How to negotiate a medical bill and set up a payment plan

    Check for errors first, research fair prices, use a calm phone script, and get the payment plan in writing.
