About

The Medical Bill & Insurance Denial Fighter Kit is a small, independent project. It exists because the paperwork for disputing a medical bill or appealing a denied insurance claim is confusing, and the deadlines are easy to miss.

The kit and this site are self-help educational material. They are not written or reviewed by lawyers, doctors, or financial advisers, and they are not legal, medical, or financial advice. No result is guaranteed.

How the information is checked

Every deadline or figure in the free guides is taken from an official or primary source — such as HealthCare.gov, CMS.gov, the U.S. Department of Labor, the IRS, the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, or KFF — and each guide lists the pages it relies on. Rules change and vary by plan and state, so always check the notice or plan documents you received.

Where to get free, personal help

  • Your state's Consumer Assistance Program or Department of Insurance (listed on HealthCare.gov).
  • The No Surprises Help Desk: 1-800-985-3059 (CMS).
  • For job-based plans, the Department of Labor's EBSA benefits advisors: 1-866-444-3272 (DOL).
